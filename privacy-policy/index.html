Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026

Starlight: Among Us Mods ("Starlight", "we", "our", or "us") is an Android application that lets users install community-made mods for Among Us. This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the app, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for processing your personal data is:

All Of Us Mods
Individual developer
Email: dev.aou.starlight@gmail.com

2. What Data Is Collected

2.1 Sign-In Data

Starlight requires you to sign in to play Among Us with your existing Among Us account. We support the following sign-in methods:

  • Google Sign-In (primary method). When you sign in with Google, we receive a Google-issued identity token confirming your Google account identity. This token is used to authenticate you against Among Us's backend services (operated by Innersloth and its game server partners) so that your existing Among Us account, purchases, and cosmetics can be used in Starlight. We do not receive or store your Google password, and we do not receive access to your Gmail, Drive, or other Google data beyond the basic identity confirmation needed for sign-in.
  • itch.io OAuth (developer/advanced option). itch.io sign-in remains available as a secondary, developer-facing option for users who have an itch.io account linked to Among Us. Basic account identifiers provided by itch.io are processed for authentication purposes only.

We do not operate our own user account system and do not store passwords for any sign-in method.

2.2 Gameplay and Multiplayer Data

To connect you to Among Us multiplayer matches, Starlight exchanges your sign-in token for a game authentication token, and shares this token with the multiplayer server (either Innersloth's official servers or a third-party server host you select) so that the server can identify your account, matchmaking status, and in-game entitlements (such as owned cosmetics).

2.3 Automatically Collected Data

When you use the app, the following data may be processed:

  • An anonymous, app-generated tracking identifier used solely to count content downloads
  • Technical data such as app version, device type, and operating system version
  • Crash and diagnostic data collected via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Advertising identifiers and interaction data processed via Google AdMob

The anonymous tracking identifier is generated on installation and can be reset by clearing the app's data. It is not linked to your real-world identity.

3. How Data Is Collected

Data is collected when you:

  • Sign in using Google or itch.io
  • Use the app to download mod content from our backend server
  • Use the app to connect to multiplayer servers
  • Use the app while crash reporting or diagnostics are enabled
  • View or interact with advertisements

6. Backend Infrastructure

Content delivery is handled by a backend server hosted with Hetzner in the United States, with Cloudflare (US East) acting as a reverse proxy. The backend does not log IP addresses, and IP logging is disabled at the proxy level.

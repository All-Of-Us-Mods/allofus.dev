Starlight brings the world of Among Us mods to your phone and your PC. It's one cross-platform mod manager: browse, install, and play community mods wherever you play.

Starlight is a companion app, not a replacement for the official game. To play, you sign in with your existing Among Us account — using Google Sign-In or, as a secondary option, itch.io — so your purchases, cosmetics, and progress carry over exactly as they do in the official app. Starlight does not create a new account for you and never sees or stores your password.
